Our story

Char's journey didn't begin with a degree or years of formal training. It began with a dream, a willingness to learn, and a heart full of passion.

Starting from scratch, she taught herself the art of baking, embracing every mistake, every little success, and every lesson along the way.

What began as a humble journey grew into something beautiful: turning simple ingredients into cakes and treats made with love, patience and creativity.

Today, every creation tells a little story of perseverance, passion, and the belief that beautiful things can grow from the courage to begin.

How to order

  1. Message us

    Send a message on Messenger, or call or text 0966 194 3736.

  2. Share your idea

    Tell us the occasion, date, theme and any peg photos you like.

  3. Confirm and reserve

    We agree on the design, size and price together. Pay the 50% downpayment to lock in your date. See the reservation policy.

  4. Pick up your cake

    Settle the remaining 50% and collect your cake fresh from Banga II, Plaridel on your chosen day.

Custom and wedding cakes need more time, so message us as early as you can.
